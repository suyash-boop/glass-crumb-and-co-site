Our Story

Flour, water, salt & a lot of patience

Crumb & Co started in a tiny Pune kitchen with one stubborn sourdough starter we named Doughlores. What began as weekend loaves for friends grew, one happy neighbour at a time, into the little bakery you see today.

We still do things the slow way. Every loaf is naturally leavened, cold-proofed overnight, and baked by hand each morning — no shortcuts, no additives, just honest bread with an open crumb and a deep, tangy crust.

Come say hello, grab a warm loaf, and taste what a little patience can do.
